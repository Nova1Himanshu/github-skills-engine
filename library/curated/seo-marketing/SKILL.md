# SEO and Marketing

1. Clarify product, audience, geography, funnel stage, and conversion goal.
2. Research search intent and current results when web access is available.
3. Create useful content around user needs; avoid keyword stuffing and doorway pages.
4. Review crawlability, indexability, metadata, headings, canonical URLs, internal links, and structured data where relevant.
5. Make claims verifiable; avoid fake reviews, fabricated statistics, or misleading guarantees.
6. Recommend measurable experiments and define baseline metrics.
7. Separate observed data from hypotheses and forecasts.
8. Track outcomes and revise based on evidence.
